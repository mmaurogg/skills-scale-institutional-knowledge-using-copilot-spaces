# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers own the user experience, interface design, and visual consistency. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with brand standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interfaces, wireframes, and prototypes
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define accessibility standards and ensure WCAG compliance
- Create and maintain design systems and style guides
- Collaborate with developers on implementation feasibility
- Iterate on designs based on user feedback and metrics

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered, accessible, and delightful products
- Reduce friction and improve user adoption
- Maintain design consistency and brand alignment across features

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with product and engineering teams
- User research findings and recommendations
- Accessibility audits and design documentation
- Collaboration in planning meetings to influence feature scope

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Validate design decisions against user needs and success metrics
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation constraints and technical feasibility
- **QA/Testing**: Provide acceptance criteria for visual and usability validation
- **Data Analysts**: Interpret user behavior data to inform design improvements

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts own the definition, implementation, and interpretation of metrics and dashboards. They partner with Product Managers and Engineering to ensure decisions are data-informed and success is measurable.

### Responsibilities
- Define key performance indicators (KPIs) aligned with business and product goals
- Design and implement data collection pipelines and dashboards
- Analyze user behavior, usage patterns, and performance metrics
- Provide insights and interpretation to support decision-making
- Support retrospectives with trend analysis and learnings
- Ensure data quality, privacy compliance, and documentation

### Goals
- Enable data-informed decision-making across the organization
- Reduce guesswork and assumption-based planning
- Support continuous improvement through measurable feedback loops

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based metrics reviews with Product and PM
- Data dashboards and trend reports
- Post-launch analysis and impact assessment
- Recommendations based on quantitative findings

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on KPI definition and interpret findings for prioritization
- **Developers**: Work with engineering on instrumentation and data quality
- **UX/UI Designers**: Share user behavior insights to inform design iterations
- **Project Managers**: Provide metrics for tracking project health and velocity

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers manage continuous integration, deployment pipelines, infrastructure automation, and release processes. They enable stable, rapid, and reliable deployments while maintaining observability and incident response capabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation workflows
- Manage deployment environments (staging, production) and infrastructure
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting systems
- Create and document rollback procedures and disaster recovery plans
- Collaborate with developers on deployability and testability requirements
- Support incident response and post-incident analysis
- Ensure security scanning and compliance checks in deployment workflows

### Goals
- Reduce deployment time and increase deployment frequency
- Achieve high system uptime and reliability
- Enable developers to ship with confidence and speed

### Typical Communication
- Deployment coordination and release planning meetings
- Infrastructure and CI/CD documentation
- On-call rotation and incident response communication
- Collaboration with developers on deployment requirements during planning

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Define deployability requirements and review CI/CD configurations
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate deployment windows and release timelines
- **QA/Testing**: Provide test environment infrastructure and assist with smoke test automation
- **Product Managers**: Communicate deployment status and rollback decisions

---

## Customer Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support Leads represent the voice of the customer throughout the product lifecycle. They bridge delivery teams and customer feedback, ensuring solutions address real user pain points and support can effectively help customers.

### Responsibilities
- Represent customer perspective in planning and design reviews
- Coordinate communication between delivery teams and support operations
- Collect, prioritize, and escalate customer pain points and feature requests
- Validate solutions during QA and beta phases
- Create support documentation and knowledge base articles
- Participate in incident response and post-incident analysis
- Monitor customer satisfaction metrics and identify trends

### Goals
- Ensure product decisions address real customer needs
- Reduce support burden through better product design
- Increase customer satisfaction and reduce escalations

### Typical Communication
- Participation in planning and retrospective meetings
- Regular customer feedback summaries and trend reports
- Pre-release validation and acceptance criteria verification
- Support playbooks and troubleshooting guides

### Key Interactions
- **Product Managers**: Provide customer insights and validate problem statements
- **Developers**: Collaborate on usability, edge cases, and support-friendly error handling
- **Project Managers**: Escalate critical customer issues and business impact
- **QA/Testing**: Represent customer scenarios in test planning and acceptance criteria

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When planning new features or projects, ensure representation from relevant personas to improve decision-making and identify interdependencies.
- Refer to the "Key Interactions" sections to facilitate better cross-functional collaboration and communication.
